Turn 2 · art direction pass

Material, not panels

Same flows, same field logic, same states as v1. What changed is the composition: cotton became the architecture, the slogan crosses the seam, and the Ankh became a drawn mark.

Why v1 read as AI
  • One rectangle inside another, repeated four times. Nothing ever crossed a boundary.
  • Form optically centred in its column — the safest possible placement.
  • Luxury asserted by spacing. No material, no texture, no photography: nothing to feel.
  • The slogan sat obediently inside its padding at a polite 34px.
  • The Ankh was a Unicode-grade glyph, not a mark with a construction.
Kept, untouched
  • Every flow and state: login, register (phone → WhatsApp → profile), recovery, all errors, cooldown, lock.
  • Radius 0, hairline fields, 2px rules, flush-start labels.
  • Country code leading the number in one control group; Western numerals in Archivo.
  • Palette proportions — gold still an accent, never a fill.
The composition
  • Cotton occupies 47% of the frame and is cropped by three of its four edges — it is not a card.
  • A second crop (folded stack) straddles the material/ivory seam and escapes the bottom edge.
  • The slogan crosses that seam: المصري on ivory, the Ankh on the line, للمصري on the cloth.
  • Form sits high, not centred; measure tightened to 404px so whitespace frames rather than pads.
  • Ankh watermark at 380px, cropped by two edges, 9% ink.
Motion · three moves only
  • Drift — 26s ambient scale on the cotton, alternating. Imperceptible per second, alive over a minute.
  • Rise — 700ms staggered fade-up on the lockup then the form rows, on first paint only.
  • Draw — the Ankh strokes itself once at verification, 900ms. That is the only celebratory motion in the product.
  • No parallax, no glow, no particles, no page-transition theatre.
A The mark — a drawn Ankh, not a glyph
Construction
A true semicircle on a 21-unit span, cut flat — then detached from the cross by a 6-unit air gap. The crossbar is deliberately off-centre: 18 units one side, 19 the other. Butt-cut terminals throughout. It is not an artifact; it is a monogram that happens to be an Ankh.

B Photography — one environment, three crops Every image below is a live drop target. Drag your own product shots on — the composition doesn't move.
Hero · the weave. Fills the panel on every screen. Shoot at 1:1 with raking side light so the weave reads as structure, not pattern. Ivory on ivory — no styling props.
Secondary · the fold. The crop that straddles the seam and escapes the frame. Folded tees or folded sheets — the subject is the crease and the stitch, never a garment silhouette.
Living with it. Bed linen drape, for the recovery and success screens. Same light, same ivory — the tee world and the linen world must look shot the same morning.
Composition rules
The material is the hero, so it is never fully contained: top edge is cut by the ivory band, left and bottom edges run past the frame. The secondary crop overlaps both the hero and the ivory ground, and is itself cut by the bottom edge. Type crosses the seam once per screen. Nothing else breaks the grid — the form column obeys it absolutely.
Placeholders are procedurally generated cotton plates, not stock photography. They are here so the composition can be judged; replace them with the real shoot. Slots share ids across frames — one drop fills every screen (reload to propagate).
